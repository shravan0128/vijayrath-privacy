Privacy Policy for VijayRath

Effective Date: November 22, 2026 | Last Updated: November 22, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to VijayRath. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring complete transparency regarding how data is handled. VijayRath is a personal intelligence and device companion dashboard designed to provide secure remote management, hardware telemetry, and assistive accessibility features.

By installing or using VijayRath, you agree to the collection, processing, and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Process & Device Permissions

To provide personal dashboard connectivity, remote device management, and assistive features, VijayRath requests access to specific Android device permissions:

A. Device Telemetry & Hardware State

  • Battery & Power (PowerManager): Monitors battery percentage, charging state, and power saver mode to provide continuous battery health reports on your companion dashboard.
  • Network Status (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, INTERNET): Monitors Wi-Fi and mobile data connection status to maintain encrypted socket communication with your private backend.

B. Hardware Sensors & Media

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required only when you trigger live audio monitoring or streaming from your companion dashboard. Audio is streamed directly over encrypted channels (WebRTC DTLS/SRTP) and is never permanently recorded, stored, or analyzed on public servers.
  • Camera (CAMERA): Required only when you trigger live video streaming or request a device snapshot from your companion dashboard. Video feeds are streamed in real time and are never stored or monetized.

C. Personal Data & Communications

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Used strictly on-device to allow you to view, synchronize, and backup your address book with your private companion console. Your contacts are never sold, rented, or shared with third-party advertisers.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS & NotificationListenerService): Displays foreground service alerts and mirrors selected device notifications to your companion console so you never miss critical updates. Notification contents are never mined for advertising.

D. Accessibility Service API (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

VijayRath uses the Android AccessibilityService API strictly to provide assistive features:

  • Assistive Navigation & Quick Shortcuts: Provides on-screen gesture notes and quick navigation shortcuts.
  • User Control: The service operates strictly on-device with prominent in-app disclosure.
  • No Data Harvesting: The Accessibility Service does NOT monitor background keystrokes, does NOT log passwords, does NOT capture payment information, and does NOT collect sensitive personal data.

E. Storage Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Accessed when the user requests file transfer, companion backup, or document viewing between the device and the dashboard. Files remain strictly under your control.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, maintain, and optimize real-time device telemetry and hardware monitoring.
  • Establish secure, encrypted companion streaming between your device and your dashboard.
  • Deliver high-priority foreground notifications to keep system services responsive.
  • Provide assistive navigation and edge shortcut functions via the Accessibility API.

We do NOT sell, trade, rent, or monetize your personal data to any third parties or advertising networks.

4. Data Security & Encryption

  • Encrypted in Transit: All communications between the App and backend servers are transmitted using Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS) and secure WebSockets (WSS).
  • WebRTC End-to-End Security: Real-time audio and video streams utilize Datagram Transport Layer Security (DTLS) and Secure Real-time Transport Protocol (SRTP).
  • Local On-Device Protection: Sensitive identifiers and credentials stored on the device are encrypted using Android standard cryptographic APIs.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Telemetry and transient session packets are processed ephemerally and discarded when the session ends.

You can immediately revoke all app permissions and erase all local app data by navigating to: Android Settings > Apps > VijayRath > Storage > Clear Data.

To request complete removal of any companion dashboard records, contact us at support@vijayrath.app. All requested records are permanently deleted within 48 hours.

6. Children's Privacy

VijayRath is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children.
